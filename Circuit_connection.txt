SMART ENERGY METER CONNECTIONS

ACS712 Current Sensor:
VCC -> Arduino 5V
GND -> Arduino GND
OUT -> Arduino A0

GSM Module:
TX -> Arduino Pin 9
RX -> Arduino Pin 10
GND -> Arduino GND
VCC -> External 5V

16x2 LCD Display:
RS -> Arduino Pin 12
EN -> Arduino Pin 11
D4 -> Arduino Pin 5
D5 -> Arduino Pin 4
D6 -> Arduino Pin 3
D7 -> Arduino Pin 2
